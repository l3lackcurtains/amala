## Entry Mechanics: React to the Confirmed Sweep

The one entry behavior he demonstrates publicly comes from his "Under The Hood" coaching example, where he diagnoses a member's NQ trade:

> "You entered before the liquidity sweep confirmed. You're anticipating instead of reacting. Let's look at what the base hit entry would have looked like..."

Three documented principles fall out of it:
- Wait for the **liquidity sweep to confirm** before entering.
- **Anticipating instead of reacting** is his #1 called-out mistake.
- Good entries are "base hit entries" — small, high-probability, repeatable.

His long-form videos follow a top-down process on camera: "Higher timeframe analysis" → "Trading setup breakdown" → "Additional trade analysis." The precise entry template beyond this is inside the Lab — not public. *(inference: the sweep-confirmation + wide-stop combination suggests he enters after a failed continuation sweep, with the stop beyond the sweep extreme — this synthesis is not stated verbatim anywhere.)*
