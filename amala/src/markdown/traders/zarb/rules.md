## Risk & Stop Rules (WASL)

1. **WASL: use a Wide Ass Stop Loss.** His signature rule — the acronym is chanted on stream and names his whole approach.
 > "WASL isn't a meme — it's a risk philosophy. Stops that are too tight get you wicked out of good trades before they ever play out."
2. **Size the stop so the trade can breathe without blowing up the account.**
 > "…how to size a stop loss that gives your trade room to breathe without blowing up your account."
3. **Tight stops are the failure mode.** The rule exists because tight stops wicked traders out of trades that were right — the stop is the bug, not the idea.
4. **Show every stop and every loss publicly.** His accountability standard: "Every decision, every WASL, shown as it happens."

## Entry Rules

5. **Wait for the liquidity sweep to confirm before entering.** The member error he dissects in his coaching examples is entering pre-confirmation.
 > "You entered before the liquidity sweep confirmed."
6. **React, don't anticipate.**
 > "You're anticipating instead of reacting."
7. **Work top-down: higher-timeframe analysis before any setup breakdown.** His analysis order on camera: "Higher timeframe analysis" → "Trading setup breakdown" → "Additional trade analysis."
8. **Drive every trade with the ICT x Volume Profile framework** — liquidity, market structure, Volume Profile, FVGs, order blocks, draw on liquidity, premium/discount.
9. **Take base-hit entries only — skip the low-probability home-run swing.**
 > "Consistent, disciplined entries beat low-probability big swings every time."
10. **Trade from a checklist.** He works from a fixed checklist; the list itself is paid-curriculum material.

## Payout & Prop-Firm Rules

11. **Two payouts per account.** His stated account secret: farm each funded account for two payouts, not one.
12. **Base hits secure payouts — don't gamble for one big payout.**
 > "You understand that base hits secure prop firm payouts. You're not here to gamble for one payout — you're here to build a process that pays you consistently for the long term."
13. **Train for the funded stage, not the challenge pass.**
 > "You know how to pass a challenge — but the funded stage is where it falls apart. You're not struggling to get in, you're struggling to stay consistent long enough to get paid. That's exactly what we fix."
14. **Diversify across prop firms.** Futures at Apex Trader Funding, Tradeify, Lucid Trading, Top One Futures; perps at Vest Exchange and MyFundedPerps.
15. **Payouts must come from live streamed trading.**
 > "Every payout came from live trading on stream — same concepts, same WASL approach we teach inside the Lab."
16. **Publish your receipts.** His payout track record is posted publicly — "No highlight reel."

## Model-Building Rules (his teaching philosophy)

17. **There is no copy/paste strategy — build your own model.**
 > "There is no copy/paste strategy. The real edge comes from how *you* use the tools — and we're going to build that together."
18. **Never clone Zarb.**
 > "The goal is never to clone how Zarb trades — it's to understand the core concepts well enough that Zarb can help you build something that fits your psychology, schedule, and style."
19. **Bring a foundation — this is not learn-to-trade-from-zero.**
 > "You should have a foundation in trading and understand basic concepts… it's for traders who are serious and ready to refine their edge."
20. **Learn the full framework before trading it.** His 23-chapter Liquidity Concepts course is the curriculum foundation "the coaching is built on top of."

## Process & Routine Rules

21. **Trade live daily, 4 hours, on stream.** His operating cadence runs on Prop Firm Match TV, Fridays included.
22. **Focus the session on the PM Kill Zone.** Dedicated PM Kill Zone streams plus a members-only PM session after the daily stream.
23. **Review trades in a group, daily-ish.** 3 live coaching calls/week, each with an "Under The Hood" segment dissecting 1–2 members' real trades.
