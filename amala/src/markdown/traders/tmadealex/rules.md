These are rules Alex states or visibly follows across his own streams and his PropFirmMatch segments. Many streams have co-hosts; only what Alex says or endorses is included. Quotes are his own words from auto-captions, lightly cleaned.

## Level & Location Rules

1. **Never short into a weekly value area low, and never long into a weekly value area high.** These are weekly pivot points where the market rotates. Wait for confirmation through them.
   > "Never short into a weekly value area low. Do not short into a weekly value area low. That is suicidal."
2. **If price can't hold above value, play the rotation to the other side.** Long at the value area low back inside value. Targets are VWAP, then POC, then the value area high.
   > "If price can't hold above value, it's going to rotate to the other side of value."
3. **Read weekly and previous-week profiles before daily levels.** Weekly levels are more powerful. A daily FVG doesn't matter where the weekly profile shows no volume.
   > "If you're not looking at weekly, monthly, previous volume profiles, you're literally trading blind."
4. **Every trade needs a liquidity target.** He won't enter without a level to go to: a POC, VWAP, value edge, open gap, or session high or low.
   > "You can't just really long it or short it. You have to have a reason to."
5. **No longs once the previous-day high has been taken** if there is no liquidity left above. "I have no targets higher."
6. **Don't enter from the middle of a range.** Wait for the edges, the stacked value levels, or 50% with confluence.
7. **Respect the walls.** Don't short into a put wall, and don't long with a call wall right above.
   > "I cannot short anything into a put wall."
8. **A 15-minute gap against you is a magnet.** Don't fight a bearish 15m gap with a long. If price inverts the 15m gap, the idea is invalid.
   > "For me invalidation is the 15-minute gap."
9. **Fade the obvious gap.** If everyone can see a gap, don't trade into it with the crowd.
   > "If my grandmother could spot an opening gap… I can't take that trade."

## Entry Rules

10. **Volume profile gives the context; an inversion gives the trigger.** Use a 1-minute IFVG at strong locations such as 50% of a higher-timeframe wick or a value edge. Use a 5-minute IFVG at weaker ones.
    > "Volume profile confirms it. Now the trigger is the inversion fair value gap."
11. **A sweep must be followed by an inversion or CISD.** A sweep of an hourly, 4-hour or session level with no inversion afterward does not qualify.
12. **Don't short right after a low is swept, and don't long right after a high is swept.** Wait for the retracement.
    > "I want to be short after an hourly high taken, not after hourly low taken."
13. **Wait for the manipulation first.** Short the squeeze higher, not the range. Long the flush lower, not the open.
    > "I want to be the guy that shorts off of a manipulation higher."
14. **Aggression has to fail before you fade it.** Look for aggression into a level that gets stopped in its path, with no continuation. Never fade a slow drift.
    > "If this low is going to get taken, those sellers have to get aggressive. They have to be rewarded for that aggression."
15. **Follow the session trend.** If buyers have controlled the session, don't try to be smarter than everyone else and short it without an inversion of the 15m gap.
16. **Don't chase; let it come.** Enter on the retest. Chasing skews the stop and ruins the R.
    > "I don't chase it. I let it come."
17. **Wait for the candle close.** His most repeated self-correction: "Never waited for a closure." He uses a 1-minute or 3-minute close for confirmation and the 15-minute close for the bigger idea.
18. **Expect a second sweep before the first inversion off a previous-day high.** The first inversion right off it rarely works.
19. **ES and NQ diverging is a warning, not a reason.** Don't short the weaker index just because it has more room. If ES and NQ give inversions in both directions, do nothing.
    > "This is just where you don't do anything."

## Stop & Management Rules

20. **Put the stop just beyond the aggressive buyers or sellers.** If price gets back through the big order you followed, you don't want the trade.
    > "Stop's going to be below this big boy, this big buyer."
21. **Go to breakeven quickly.** With bigger size, take the "free trade" on the first pullback. Never let a $1,000 winner turn into a loss inside a bearish gap.
22. **Take the low-hanging fruit.** Bank the nearer target instead of holding for the far one. Don't be "a dick for a tick" by holding for the last few points.
    > "Base hits, base hits, take my money."
23. **After a valid stop, re-enter with more size only if the setup has more confirmation now.**
    > "I'll take the stop and enter back in with double the size… because I'll have more confirmation."
24. **Size down once you're green.** Take later trades with smaller size so a loss is a paper cut.
    > "I've already made money. I don't need to take this long."
25. **Protect a green day.** If you're already up and the market turns choppy, stop participating.
    > "I'm no longer going to participate because I am already up… That's how you get past evals."
26. **Two stops in the same choppy market means walk away.**
    > "This is where you have to just walk away… this price action is horrible."

## Sizing & Account Rules

27. **Size by account type.** On evals he risks 50–100% of the drawdown to pass in one or two trades. On funded accounts it's 25% per trade.
    > "When I'm trading the evals, I trade between 50% to 100%… when I'm funded, I trade 25% of the account in each trade. So like five micros essentially."
28. **Micros, not minis, in this market.** Use 1–5 micros on funded accounts, and one mini at most on a 150K eval.
    > "You don't need more than three to five micros in this market."
29. **Grade every idea: A+ ideas go on funded accounts, C-grade ideas only go on evals.**
    > "This is a C-grade trade… I would never take this on a funded account ever."
30. **On wild days, trade one account at a time.** Copy trade the stack only once conditions calm down, usually closer to 10 AM.
    > "My style is aggressive and I used to copy that to 20 accounts and completely lose."
31. **Never cross-risk a whole stack at once.** Keep a futures stack and a Vest stack separate, and diversify across firms.
32. **Use your own data feed.** Lead the copier from your own Rithmic data so a firm's outage doesn't hit you.
33. **Avoid trailing drawdown.** He blew a winning account to a trailing drawdown pullback and calls those rules predatory. He prefers end-of-day or static drawdown.
    > "How can you be in a winning trade and lose? Like that doesn't make any sense."
34. **No daily-loss-limit add-ons, and 50% consistency over 40%.** He chooses account rules that let him trade his own way.
35. **Take payouts as they come.** Small, frequent withdrawals beat building big buffers.
    > "I'm not building buffers. I'm just taking out my money as it comes in."
36. **Don't copy his trades. Learn the setup.** His stops and targets reflect his risk tolerance, not yours.

## Timing Rules

37. **Don't trade the open.** If you're not already profitable, the first 15–30 minutes aren't for you.
    > "Do not trade the open, guys. The open is not for you."
38. **Trade the hours with the highest probability: 10, 11 and 2.**
    > "The probability is in our favor at 10:00 a.m., at 11:00, and at 2."
39. **Expect the new hour to manipulate.** Don't hold through a new hourly open, and look for the 10 AM and 2 PM sweeps.
40. **Asia gets office hours.** He usually takes one trade and is done, with no perps in Asia.
    > "The more you stay at the casino, the more you'll lose. So with Asia session, you need to have office hours."
41. **Read the lunch box late.** The real move comes in roughly its last 20 minutes, and price should retrace to 50% and the VWAP before taking the low.
42. **On extreme days, only trade evals.** When NQ is down several percent, it's a "pass your evals" day, not a funded-account day.

## Mindset Rules

43. **Patience beats activity.** You don't have to take every trade or even trade every day.
    > "Patience will change your life. It's that simple."
44. **Don't break your rules.**
    > "That's why you don't break your rules, chat. You can't break your rules."
45. **Care about being green, not being right.** "Choose your draw on liquidity." Ego-based trading loses.
46. **Don't fixate on the target.** His worst blow-up came from refusing to let go of a target and revenge-sizing into it.
    > "I became too fixated on hitting my target and I got completely smoked."
