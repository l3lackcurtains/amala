## The Backstory

From the [Trading Nut interview](https://tradingnut.com/daniel-holmes/) — [01:50]:
*"At 29, depressed and broke, I literally typed 'top earning jobs in the world' into Google and
found trading."* He became obsessed, then spent years blowing accounts, self-sabotaging, and
overcomplicating his charts before the price-action reset.

## Documented Lessons (Trading Nut, with timestamps)

1. **Kill the indicators** — [07:13]: *"Adding indicators like EMAs and Bollinger Bands threw away
 two years of my progress."* Clean price action over systems.
2. **Longevity beats intensity** — [09:34]: *"Longevity in the game is key; if you show up with
 intent to do better than yesterday, you will improve."*
3. **Blowing accounts is tuition** — [10:53]: *"With prop trading, blowing accounts is just part
 of the cycle to get to the payouts."*
4. **Experience enforces rules** — [12:30]: *"Experience is the only thing that actually forces
 you to stick to your trading rules."*
5. **Your thesis has a deadline** — [16:19]: *"If what you anticipated to happen doesn't happen,
 you are wrong — start managing the trade immediately."*
6. **Expect nothing** — [18:11]: *"Expect the worst on every trade. The market owes you nothing."*
7. **Detach from single accounts** — [28:28]: cycle through multiple prop accounts; never get
 emotionally attached to any one of them.
