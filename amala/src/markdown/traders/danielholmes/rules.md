## The 2-Day Prop Challenge Method

From his [Trading Nut interview](https://tradingnut.com/daniel-holmes/) — [28:28]:

1. **Aggressive challenge passing:** on a 50K challenge (Topstep cited), he **risks the entire
 account on the very first trade** to make ~50% in a single day — passing in two days instead
 of grinding for weeks.
2. **No emotional attachment to a single account:** he manages **multiple prop accounts in
 cycles** — working a batch, then rotating.
3. **The prop cycle:** *"Prop firm trading is a cycle of blowing accounts, getting funded,
 getting a payout, and repeating"* — [25:53]. Blowing an eval is treated as part of the cost
 structure, not a failure event.

> ⚠️ This is his documented, controversial approach for **evaluation phases only** — extremely
> high variance. It is not his funded-account trading style. — [Trading Nut](https://tradingnut.com/daniel-holmes/)

## Engulfing Entry Rules (documented)

1. **Never enter on a breakout candle alone** — wait for the engulfing candle or a strong closure
 confirmation on the higher timeframe ([Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DF5NXy7TeY_/)).
2. **Enter with a pending stop above/below the engulfing candle's extreme** — the trade triggers
 itself, no manual chasing ([TikTok — danielholmestrades](https://www.tiktok.com/@danielholmestrades/video/7500427483565526293)).
3. **Three consecutive bullish candles are not a bottom** — require lower-timeframe engulfing
 confirmation before reversal entries ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/61594911931424/videos/1897040161662252/)).
4. **A full engulfing after no follow-through is the flip** — he reads it as the structure shift
 signalling the auction turned ([Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXHIl1ujERa/)).

**Stacked with the breakout model:** break on the 15m → retest → demand the engulfing / strong
closure → enter via pending stop at the engulfing extreme → 1:1 target.
