## The Core Setup: 15-Minute Candle Close & Retest Breakout (Gold)

Documented in [his $106K/month strategy breakdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2zTSzqVLT3E)
and the [Trading Nut interview](https://tradingnut.com/daniel-holmes/):

1. **Timeframe & session:** 15-minute chart, focused on **London session breakouts** on Gold.
2. **The break:** identify a key support/resistance level; trade the candle that **closes**
 through it — not the wick. Most breakout traders lose by *entering too early, chasing
 candles, and forcing confirmations*.
3. **The retest:** wait for price to come back and retest the broken level.
4. **The confirmation:** a **wick flip** on the retest — rejection away from the old level.
5. **Entry:** enter after the flip, joining the **high-volume momentum** leg.
6. **Target:** ride momentum for a **1:1 risk-to-reward** — he takes the consistent 1R rather
 than holding for multiples.

## Engulfing Candles — His Confirmation Layer

Daniel Holmes uses **engulfing candles as the trigger that turns a breakout idea into a real
entry**. Documented across his public reels and posts:

- **Multi-timeframe confluence:** he waits for the alignment of timeframes, not one candle.
 His documented example: *"we have a breakout on the 15-minute timeframe, a one-hour engulfing
 candle, and a strong 30-minute closure"* — the higher-timeframe engulfing is the green light
 on the lower-timeframe breakout ([Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DF5NXy7TeY_/)).
- **Pending-stop entries at the engulfing extreme:** instead of entering on the candle itself, he
 **sets pending buy/sell stops above/below the engulfing candle** and lets price trigger him 
 removing the guesswork and the early-entry problem ([TikTok — danielholmestrades](https://www.tiktok.com/@danielholmestrades/video/7500427483565526293)).
- **Three bullish candles ≠ a bottom:** he warns that consecutive bullish candles alone mean
 nothing — he requires **engulfing confirmation on the lower timeframe** before entering a
 reversal, specifically to avoid fakeouts
 ([Facebook — Daniel Holmes](https://www.facebook.com/61594911931424/videos/1897040161662252/)).
- **Engulfing as the exhaustion flip:** when buyers are heavy at the top with no follow-through,
 a **full engulfing candle** is the signal the auction has turned — he reads it as the structure
 shift, not a prediction ([Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXHIl1ujERa/)).

**In practice, stacked with his breakout model:** break on the 15m → wait for the retest → demand
the engulfing / strong-closure confirmation on the higher frame → enter via a pending stop at the
engulfing extreme → 1:1 target.
