## Her story and identity

- Krista (Krista Fudge), mom of three young kids.
 > "I've just created this, the YouTube name Fit Mom Trader just based on my background. So I, um, owned a gym for about 15 years. I did online nutrition coaching. Like that's my background, studied kinesiology. Um, so fitness is a big part of my life. And then I'm a mom. I have three young kids."
- Found futures trading through Joey Morgan posting about NQ: "he started posting about this NQ futures trading stuff... started posting about like time freedom and working for an hour a day and I made 500 bucks and I'm like, I want that."
- Started YouTube for accountability after a live-trading bootcamp:
 > "you were challenging the group to live trade live on YouTube more as a way just to have some accountability... Cause even if you have 10 people watching, you know, you're going to take fewer dumb trades that way. So I started a YouTube channel in May right after that bootcamp."
- Her channel description is her brand ethos: "No hype. No Lambos. Just real futures trading, consistency, and continuous improvement." And: "I became profitable early in my trading journey — but I'm still learning every single day."

## Boring is the edge

- Her most-viewed strategy video frames it: "my trading strategy is boring, but it made me $100,000 in 2 months."
- Community post: "If trading feels boring, you're leveling up. Boring trading is often profitable trading."
- Her site pitch aligns: consistent prop-firm payouts *without changing your strategy* — the method is consistency, not new setups.

## Tilt control

- She has a dedicated video on tilt ("Can you trade without tilting? Try this.") covering the psychological triggers that lead to tilt and a practical framework to regain control of your mindset.
- On overtrading, revenge trading & emotional triggers: her structural anti-tilt tools are alerts instead of screen-staring ("if my alert doesn't go off, then I don't trade"), account lockouts ("I'll lock it all the way until the Tokyo session. So I'm not distracted."), and a hard 2-loss daily stop.
- "The lockout button protects my profits, my mental capital, and my time."

## Honest about failures

- Her site headline: the coaching method that took her "From Blowing Accounts To $28K In A Single Week, Without Spending All Day On The Charts."
- Her free course includes "real live trades, including the day I blew an account and came back."
- She names her own biggest failure mode: chasing payout requirements led to blown accounts.
- Admits open weaknesses on air: "What I'm working on, I think, is how to manage my trade. So I don't have a really clear-cut system on when I move my stop loss to break even, when I take partials, when I take my full profit target."
- Tried and rejected other systems without shame: "I definitely went down the rabbit hole of every YouTube strategy there is. You know, I checked out ICT and I was like, well, that's confusing. I don't understand that. Checked out like RSI diversions is I don't understand that. So I've stuck with the initial balance and then a couple other things because it just made sense to me."

- Her position-sizing video frames chapters like "Planning for losing streaks" and "Emotional discipline" — sizing is psychological protection: "the psychological benefits of trading a size that fits your account balance."

## Parent-trader time management

 > "I think in terms of like balancing trading and kids is, I actually do trade Asia very successfully, but obviously it's hard with kids. Because in the evenings, that's when we're doing homework and taking them to sports practice and dinner and bath and bedtime and all that stuff. So if my face is in my phone the whole time, it's really hard."

- Solution: alerts + lockouts + short sessions + Fridays off ("I'm pretty spent by the end of the week") + choosing a trading session that fits your life.
