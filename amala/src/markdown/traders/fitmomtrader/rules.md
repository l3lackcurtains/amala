## Risk & position sizing

1. **Fixed-dollar risk per trade: $200** (on a 50K prop account with $2K drawdown), at most 2 losses a day; size is derived from the stop with the position tool — never from a fixed contract count.
2. **Size contracts from the stop distance, not from a fixed contract count.** Her position tool converts stop width into contracts. Her video "Why Position Size Beats All Other Secrets": "the real secret to long-term success lies in managing your risk... the psychological benefits of trading a size that fits your account balance."
3. **"How many contracts do you trade?" is the wrong question.** Sizing is a function of account and stop, not a number to copy.
4. **Trade micros (MNQ) to pass evaluations and protect capital.**

## Daily loss limits & stop-trading triggers

5. **Maximum 2 losing trades per day, then stop.**
6. **No trading in the last hour of the NY session.** Her "when to avoid trading" list: "Stay out: choppy/flat-VWAP days, unconfirmed breakouts (wait for the candle close), after the daily loss limit, the last hour of the NY session, lunch/holiday low volume, CPI/PPI/FOMC/NFP days."
7. **Skip news days: CPI, PPI, FOMC, NFP.**
8. **Skip choppy / flat-VWAP days and lunch / holiday low-volume periods.**
9. **After the daily loss limit is hit, the day is over.**

## Entries & confirmation

10. **Two setups only: Failed Auction and Trend Continuation off the Initial Balance.** "There's two ways that I trade it: either a rejection of the high or the low — a failed auction — or a trend continuation trade." She calls them her two A+ setups.
11. **Wait for confirmation before entering — don't trade every breakout.**
 > "Don't trade every breakout — look for retests, liquidity grabs, and price action confirmation around the Initial Balance levels before entering your trades."
 And on breakouts: wait for the candle close.
12. **Failed auction entry: break outside IB fails and closes back inside → target the IB mid, stop beyond the recent swing.**
13. **Trend continuation entry: retrace to IB mid (VWAP nearby = extra confluence) → enter with the trend, target the opposite IB extreme, stop beyond the recent swing.**
14. **Choose the setup by day type.** "It's been more of a trending market, so I'm taking a lot of retracements to the IB mid and trend continuation trades. If it's more choppy, then I'll take the failed auction."
15. **Asia/Tokyo variant: mark the 18:00–20:00 ET range, then trade a failed sweep or a confirmed breakout + retest; targets prior levels or ~3R.**

## Session & routine discipline

16. **Alert-based execution — no alert, no trade.**
 > "I'm also not just staring at the charts the whole time. I typically trade by setting an alert. So if my alert doesn't go off, then I don't trade. If it goes off, then I take a look real quick and I trade it."
17. **Use the lockout button.** "The lockout button protects my profits, my mental capital, and my time." She locks accounts until the Tokyo session so she isn't distracted, takes one or two trades, then locks up again.
18. **Limit trades per session: "one trade, maybe two" then lock.**
19. **Trade Monday–Thursday; take Fridays off.** "I'm pretty spent by the end of the week."
20. **Stream live for accountability.** "Even if you have 10 people watching, you know, you're going to take fewer dumb trades that way."

## Prop-firm tactics

21. **Prefer evaluations over instant funding** — though she still reviews instant-funding options for her audience.
22. **Do NOT chase payout requirements — it blew her accounts.** Chasing payout requirements is what she names as her own biggest failure mode.
23. **Cut evaluation spending and keep accounts longer.**
24. **Know the firm's rulebook before trading it** — evaluation and drawdown types, consistency rules, and what each firm actually requires.
25. **Use payouts + lockouts together.** Payout requests are made while the account is locked from further trading.
26. **Firms she uses/promotes:** Tradeify, Lucid Trading, FundedNext, My Funded Futures, TradeDay, Alpha Futures, Top One Futures, The Futures Desk, FuturesElite — with Lucid and Tradeify credited as her main payout sources.
