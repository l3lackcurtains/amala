## The core system: 60-minute Initial Balance (her "bread and butter")

Her own words:

> "So the initial balance is the first 60 minutes of the session and it can be applied to New York, London, Tokyo, whatever. But I trade specifically in the New York session. So from 930 to 1030, you mark off the high and the low of that first 60 minutes. And that's the range of the session. You're using that to kind of set the tone for the day. Um, so basically it's like a 60 minute orb and there's two ways that I trade it either a rejection of the high or the low, which is the fancy term they call it as a failed auction. Um, or you can take a trend continuation trade."

A third-party strategy-lab breakdown of her most-viewed video ("my trading strategy is boring, but it made me $100,000 in 2 months") formalizes it: "Initial balance (IB) = high and low of the first 60 minutes of the NY session, 09:30–10:30 ET; IB mid = 50% of it. It is her main ('bread and butter') setup."

## Setup 1 — Failed Auction (rejection of IB high/low)

- "A rejection of the high or the low, which is the fancy term they call it as a failed auction."
- Mechanization from her video: "Setup 1, failed auction: price breaks outside the IB, cannot hold, rejects and closes back inside; enter toward the IB mid, target the IB mid, stop beyond the recent swing low/high."
- Her video "This Initial Balance Setup Has a Statistical Edge" breaks down an IB failed-auction trade using Edgeful market statistics, key levels and ES confluence — entry, trade management, and the confluences that made it high-probability.

## Setup 2 — Trend Continuation (retrace to IB mid / VWAP)

- "So like maybe it's a bullish trend and it breaks the IB high initial balance high comes back down and it retraces to like the midpoint or to VWAP. And then you take a trend continuation trade."
- Day-type conditional selection: "It's been more of a trending market. So I'm taking a lot of retracements to the IB mid and trend continuation trades. If it's more choppy, then I'll take the failed auction. So it depends. That's the cool thing about the strategy is you can adjust to the market conditions."
- Mechanization from her video: "Setup 2, trend continuation: the IB trended one way; price returns to the IB mid (anchored 09:30 VWAP nearby is extra confluence), rejects, enter with the trend, target the IB low (high), stop beyond the recent swing high (low)."

## Trade management (stops, partials, targets)

- Stops: "beyond the recent swing low/high" for both IB setups.
- Targets: failed auction → IB mid; trend continuation → opposite IB extreme; Asia variant → prior levels or ~3R.
- Her own admitted gap: "What I'm working on, I think, is how to manage my trade. So I don't have a really clear-cut system on when I move my stop loss to break even, when I take partials, when I take my full profit target."
- Her IB breakdown video teaches stop placement, partials, and scale-outs, with the pro tip: "Don't trade every breakout — look for retests, liquidity grabs, and price action confirmation around the Initial Balance levels before entering your trades."
