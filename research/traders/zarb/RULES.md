## Risk & Stop-Loss Rules (the WASL system)

1. **Use a Wide Ass Stop Loss — WASL is the rule, not a joke.** His signature rule, named on the landing page and chanted on stream.
   > "WASL isn't a meme — it's a risk philosophy. Stops that are too tight get you wicked out of good trades before they ever play out." *(iamzarb.com — WASL Gang section)*
2. **Size the stop so the trade can breathe without blowing up the account.**
   > "Inside the Lab, we talk through exactly how to size a stop loss that gives your trade room to breathe without blowing up your account — the same approach behind every payout below." *(iamzarb.com)*
3. **Tight stops are the failure mode to eliminate.** The entire justification of WASL is that tight stops cause premature stop-outs on trades that would have worked. *(iamzarb.com)*
4. **Show every stop and every loss publicly.** His accountability standard for himself: every decision and every WASL is visible on stream.
   > "Every decision, every WASL, shown as it happens." *(iamzarb.com — coach bio)*
