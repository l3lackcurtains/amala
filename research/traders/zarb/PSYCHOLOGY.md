> "Prop firm trading is about not blowing up. Consistent, disciplined entries beat low-probability big swings every time. We build that discipline together." *(iamzarb.com)*

## WASL is a patience philosophy, not just a stop rule

> "WASL isn't a meme — it's a risk philosophy. Stops that are too tight get you wicked out of good trades before they ever play out." *(iamzarb.com)*

**[inference]** Psychologically, WASL is the antidote to the fear that gets traders stopped out of winners: accept a larger dollar risk per trade in exchange for the emotional room to let the thesis play out. It trades account-space for peace of mind.

## Anticipation vs. reaction — the core behavioral error he coaches

> "You're anticipating instead of reacting." *(iamzarb.com — Under The Hood demo dialogue)*

He frames trading discipline as waiting for the market to confirm (liquidity sweep) before acting — the same reactive discipline theme across his teaching.
