## 1. Method & concepts

**Overall identity and framework**
- He calls himself an ICT trader: "because I'm an ICT trader, I teach specifically..." (_8b5w852Yis [389:40]). He says he is "not really a reversal trader. I'm trading the continuations" (_8b5w852Yis [402:20]). Elsewhere: "since I'm a continuation trader" (sl4Phv8Gadc [137:20]) and "I'm kind of a momentum or a continuation trader" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [120:40]).
- His view is that the "real golden ticket is ICT mixed with something else". He was adding volume (bubbles/CVD) to the ICT model "to help bulletproof this even more" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [119:00], [122:00]).
- Learning path: supply & demand first, then a few months as a Bookmap trader "with some success", then ICT. ICT taught him "why candles are printing the way they are" without looking at level-2 (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [118:00]–[119:00]).
- By Oct 2026 the stack had changed: "You know what I trade now? Gamma levels and order flow... and obviously ICT". He still looks at 15-minute FVGs and uses the 1-minute for entry (9AhgcNBSW9g [614:00]).
- He says time and the new tools (tape, gamma, CVD) made him better. Five months earlier he "would have just been like, oh we should take the high based on 80% chance" (9AhgcNBSW9g [617:20]–[617:40]).
- The model is shared with his partner/mentor "Jacob". It rests on 3 years of backtest data and edgeful stats. The shared indicators are called "our Jacob and Alex indicator" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [119:40]; nfXb6bqOwHk [165:40]; sl4Phv8Gadc [171:40]).
- In Dec 2025 he and Jacob moved some trades to ES "if we see that ES looks better that day", after running edgeful stats on the model (sl4Phv8Gadc [171:20]–[171:40]).

**Trapped participants (his core logic)**
- "All a V-shaped recovery is is trapped participants below. All an IFVG is is trapped participants below." A bullish IFVG means "sellers getting trapped below you and the market literally inversing an area of aggressive selling" (9AhgcNBSW9g [614:40]–[615:00]).
- Mechanics: trapped sellers who close their shorts have to buy, which adds to the buying and drives the squeeze (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [143:00]–[143:40]; Pcv1ZQg7LyM [149:40]–[150:40]).
- He wants markets with gaps: "I want there to be trapped sellers or buyers". He calls gapless back-and-forth price "efficient", meaning it is taking every order, and says it is "hard to trade" (_8b5w852Yis [405:40]–[406:00]).
- For an explosive move "someone needs to be squeezed". A low-volume grind up with no bubbles means passive absorption, not real buying (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [123:20]–[124:00]).

**CISD / change in state of delivery**
- His definition: "mostly just a set of up or down candles that get closed on". It does not wait for a break of structure, which makes it "one of the fastest ways to get into a trade". Often it forms before an FVG/IFVG, and only an order block forms earlier (_8b5w852Yis [398:20]–[399:00]).
- A CISD is only high-probability at a point of interest: previous-day high/low, 50% of the range, or higher-timeframe PD arrays (15m, 1h, 4h) (_8b5w852Yis [399:20]–[400:00]).
- Stacking: V-shaped recovery + IFVG + CISD + breaker ("unicorn") + delivery from an HTF PDA is "all A+" (_8b5w852Yis [402:00]–[402:20]).
- Inside the model, momentum is often already running when he enters and there is no FVG. So he uses a 30-second or 1-minute CISD for the continuation entry (nfXb6bqOwHk [140:00]–[140:40]).

**Fib, premium/discount, golden pocket**
- He fibs the previous day's high-to-low candle, not the current swing, because the new day trades into it. If you have a short bias, "you don't really want to see price trading above 50% of yesterday's price" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [103:20]–[104:20]).
- The golden pocket is 0.62–0.79. He prefers 0.79 (nfXb6bqOwHk [136:20]). He also fibs the opening range from 9:30, looking at 0.79 and 0.886 (nfXb6bqOwHk [138:00]–[138:40]).
- Losing 50% of the previous-day fib and then a retest into a 5-minute gap gives short confluence (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [109:40]; [111:00]).
- If buyers push back into premium and sellers can't hold price out of it, he looks for continuation (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [154:00]–[154:20]). He credits Trader Kane for premium/discount (and standard deviations at all-time highs) (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [165:20]–[166:00]).

**Daily manipulation / distribution levels**
- He uses a "1D +D" tool built from ~600 sessions. It projects where the daily candle manipulates and distributes. He wants price to run from the midnight open into a manipulation zone and then show a higher-timeframe (3m/5m) inversion out of it (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [104:40]–[106:00]).

**Volume imbalances, wicks, "devil's mark"**
- A volume imbalance is an area where candle bodies don't overlap, and it matters most on higher timeframes. He treats them as "liquidity targets" and magnets (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [152:40]–[153:20]; _8b5w852Yis [363:40]).
- If price takes a daily volume imbalance and closes back above it, that is acceptance and he expects continuation (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [153:40]–[154:20]).
- A 15-minute candle that has only traded one way will "put in the body" later, so the wick side gets retested. He uses this "wick retest on a 15-minute candle" for continuation entries (_8b5w852Yis [381:00]; [387:00]).
- No-wick candles: price usually comes back to take the missing wick. On the 15-minute "I will literally hunt that as a hawk... they call it the devil's mark" (nfXb6bqOwHk [162:40]–[163:40]).
- He watches 15-minute opens and closes closely because "they do things at their open and close" (wick placement) (nfXb6bqOwHk [155:40]).
- TTrades "candle 3": after candle 2 sweeps candle 1's low, candle 3 should deliver. He admits this knowledge can stop him taking his own continuation short (nfXb6bqOwHk [140:40]–[141:20]; sl4Phv8Gadc [139:40]).

**SMT and relative strength**
- He uses NQ vs ES SMT on several timeframes (15m, 1h, 4h, daily) and has an indicator for YM SMTs, which "sometimes... work" (sl4Phv8Gadc [119:20]–[120:00]; [142:40]; [159:20]–[159:40]).
- He exited a long because NQ swept a 15-minute high and ES did not: "a 15-minute potential SMT... I don't want to f— around and find out" (sl4Phv8Gadc [149:20]–[149:40]).
- He trades NQ off ES, ES off NQ, or ES off ES, choosing the instrument that sits at the cleaner level (cZPjYzTO0gw [644:00]).
- He prefers NQ and ES to be in sync at their gamma levels. If ES is at its put wall but NQ is far from its own, he gets "annoyed" and may trade ES instead (cZPjYzTO0gw [638:20]–[639:40]).
- On YM he agreed with Zach that it is not much use for relative strength (nfXb6bqOwHk [147:00]) (speaker inferred).

**Sessions as liquidity (Asia/London/NY)**
- "London's enough. We don't have to even go back for Asia" (sl4Phv8Gadc [118:40]; nfXb6bqOwHk [126:00]).
- When Asia/London lows are left untaken and ES is still far above them, "that gives me a clue that we most likely want to see lower" (nfXb6bqOwHk [125:20]–[125:40]).
- An A+ NY continuation day: Asia sets the high and low, London sits in the middle, NY sweeps both and then continues (_8b5w852Yis [362:20]–[363:00]).
- After big news "you leave a clear draw on liquidity" and price will "manipulate you before they give it to you" (sl4Phv8Gadc [127:40]).
- "Seek and destroy" days (high swept, then low swept) are typical of early January, which he calls a reversal-type month (sl4Phv8Gadc [159:40]–[160:00]; [166:20]).

**Volume bubbles (custom CVD/delta indicator)**
- Built for the TMade Discord. It plots only large or unusual orders ("institutional"), filtered by size. It is built from delta, and his claim is "there's no lag" (sl4Phv8Gadc [119:00]; [131:20]; Pcv1ZQg7LyM [120:20]; [129:20]–[129:40]).
- Reading it: a big buying bubble at a breakout with no continuation means a passive seller is absorbing, which is a reversal tell. You don't need to see a red bubble (sl4Phv8Gadc [131:20]–[131:40]).
- An old big buyer zone gets "protected" on retests and acts as support/resistance (sl4Phv8Gadc [132:00]; Pcv1ZQg7LyM [137:00]–[137:40]).
- "You can't get this indicator and just short bubbles... You still have to have the analysis" (sl4Phv8Gadc [134:00]). It is meant to sit inside ICT logic. For example, sellers trapped at an IFVG show up visually (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [130:00]–[131:00]).
- Uses: confirming bias, stop placement (just beyond the bubble), and targets (no bubbles in the path means price can sweep through) (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [127:00]; [131:40]; [132:20]–[133:00]).
- Development: they planned a candle/oscillator version to spot divergences, where price continues but volume falls (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [121:20]–[121:40]).

**Gamma levels (GexLab, built with Yush) — Oct 2026**
- The three levels are put wall, call wall and zero gamma. "What you're looking for is... if [they] are going to be acting as support or resistance" (9AhgcNBSW9g [647:20]).
- Zero gamma trick: if price holds the put wall and "blasts" through zero gamma before reaching the call wall, then a pullback to zero gamma is support. "That is the most gangster way to use this thing" (9AhgcNBSW9g [653:20]–[653:40]; [647:40]–[648:00]).
- Walls are where "market makers are hedging their positions". They are not value-area levels, but "if it lines up with a value area low/high it's even more powerful. That's the secret sauce" (9AhgcNBSW9g [640:40]–[641:00]).
- Walls move during the session as dealers re-hedge. A white trail shows how long a wall has held, which he likens to "a snail trail" (cZPjYzTO0gw [630:40]; 9AhgcNBSW9g [699:40]).
- Asia gamma levels are cleaner because market makers can't reposition. "You hit a G1, you can melt 300 points... you might lose one out of four trades" (cZPjYzTO0gw [653:00]–[653:20]; [671:40]–[672:00]).
- Simplified: gamma is "support resistance levels where bigger participants are going to get involved because they will lose money if they don't" (cZPjYzTO0gw [634:40]–[635:00]).

**VWAPs, volume profile, other levels**
- He anchors three VWAPs: 9:30 NY open, 6 PM ET overnight/session open, and 12:00 ET (the "lunchbox hour") (9AhgcNBSW9g [675:40]; [670:40]–[671:20]).
- He doesn't use VWAP bands: "too much stuff on my chart" (9AhgcNBSW9g [678:20]).
- VWAP is a bias filter. To short he wants price back below VWAP: "it just gives me confidence that I'm on the right path" (nfXb6bqOwHk [141:20]; [136:40]).
- Volume profile: weekly and session VAH/VAL/POC. "Value area low moving lower is not a good thing cuz we're accepting value lower" (cZPjYzTO0gw [637:00]; [709:20]) (speaker inferred for the last quote; Yush was also talking).
- Daily-wick quarters ("cheat code"): take a big daily wick that swept liquidity, split it into 0.25/0.5/0.75 and use those lines on the 5-minute chart. "Don't say TMade Alex never gave you sauce" (9AhgcNBSW9g [671:40]–[672:40]).
- AMD indicator (accumulation/manipulation/distribution). It auto-maps timeframes: 1m chart shows the hourly, 5m shows the 4H, 15m shows the daily. Once the range high or low is taken, he trades back toward the mean of the range (cZPjYzTO0gw [602:40]–[606:40]).
- Order-book tool: plots the largest resting orders within ±120 NQ points. He is testing whether to skip trading when the biggest orders sit right at price, which suggests chop (cZPjYzTO0gw [679:40]–[680:40]).

## 2. Setup & entry criteria

**The TMade model (NY AM, Dec 2025–Feb 2026)**
- No trades before 10:00–10:15. "I like to wait for the market to kind of tell me what it wants to do" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [102:00]; nfXb6bqOwHk [111:40]).
- He trades the 10–11 window: "I like to trade between 10 and 11" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [134:00]). He likes to see where price is at the 10:15 15-minute candle (sl4Phv8Gadc [126:20]–[126:40]).
- "My model is when 10 a.m. opens, I wait for it to pull back to just trade that continuation" (_8b5w852Yis [376:40]). The 10 AM candle sweeping the previous 4H candle, followed by a 5-minute inversion, is the "most basic" ICT reversal entry. He fades that crowd on continuation days (nfXb6bqOwHk [131:40]–[132:00]).
- He follows the model even when it is uncomfortable: "Some of these trades are scary because we've extended very far down... but I just have to follow my rules, my model" (_8b5w852Yis [377:00]).
- Two-trade structure: "usually we teach two trades". He takes up to three only if one of the first two was breakeven (nfXb6bqOwHk [165:40]).
- Second-trade edge (his backtest claims): the second trade wins about 80%. Over 3 years the first trade alone made about $170K, and adding the second took it over $400K. Skipping the first trade and taking only the second "is not as profitable" (nfXb6bqOwHk [165:40]–[167:20]; [169:00]).
- Why the second trade works: "we were right about the first trade... the market wasn't ready". He keeps the first trade lighter, sometimes de-risked, and on certain weekdays (from 3 years of data) he doubles or triples size on trade two (nfXb6bqOwHk [166:00]–[166:40]).
- Taking the first trade is required: "if I don't take the first trade, sometimes that second trade doesn't even come" (nfXb6bqOwHk [168:40]).
- Fridays: "for our model, we don't really take trades on Fridays". Lately on Fridays they look only for about $150 to log a winning day (sl4Phv8Gadc [150:20]–[150:40]).

**The 9 AM setup ("a little secret")**
- "9:00 a.m. is one of the easiest trades you could find." If 9:00 opens near the 8 AM hour's high or low, wait for a sweep of it. Then look for a 1-minute or 3-minute inversion back into the range (sl4Phv8Gadc [110:00]; [120:00]–[120:40]).
- Worked example: 9:00 sweeps the 8 AM high (with the 8:30 data-wick highs already taken), a 3-minute IFVG forms, the retest comes to the tick, and price sells through a 15-minute gap to the London low (sl4Phv8Gadc [120:40]–[121:40]).
- He suggested Zach run his midnight model from 9:00 too: "everything's liquidity", with midnight/POC as magnets (sl4Phv8Gadc [122:00]–[122:40]).

**Targeting 15-minute gaps (the "15-minute setup")**
- "Anytime I'm targeting a 15-minute gap, I want to get in the 15-minute candle that sweeps, and I want... the inversion to be inside that wick, because then I could trade the body into that gap" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [113:20]).
- The Topstep combine pass (the YxoBfqOn7Do clip): "just tapped a 15-minute. I'm back in... There's a 15-minute gap here that just formed" (YxoBfqOn7Do [00:00]; sl4Phv8Gadc [163:40]).
- His reasons for that reversal long: ES was at its previous-day high with VWAP there, and NQ was at prior resistance plus a 15-minute gap. "When you're trying to pass an eval, can you get a couple points out of this trade?" (sl4Phv8Gadc [164:20]–[164:40]).
- He prefers price to tag 50% of a zone and then displace, though ICT says a shallow tap with displacement is stronger (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [140:00]–[140:40]).

**A+ definition (stacked confluence)**
- An example A+ short: an extended run-up, the previous-day fib golden pocket (0.62–0.79), and 4H + 1H + 15m FVGs stacked ("a stacked amount of sellers"). He would have taken it before 10 AM: "when I see an A+ setup, I definitely want to get into it" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [102:00]; [104:20]–[104:40]).
- A V-shape + IFVG + CISD + breaker + HTF PDA together is "all A+" (_8b5w852Yis [402:20]).
- He skips the first 1-minute inversion at the open if price never actually tapped the zone, because "we've seen that happen so many times", meaning one more sweep before the move (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [100:20]; [106:00]–[106:20]).
- Confirmation filters for longs: "this closure above all these down candles with VWAP being below makes it impossible for me to short" (sl4Phv8Gadc [136:20]–[136:40]).

**Gold 8 PM Asia model ("GC strat")**
- Asia open (8 PM ET): mark the high and low of the previous 15-minute candle. Wait for a sweep of either side. Then wait for a 30-second or 1-minute inversion to the other side, i.e. the first presented FVG/IFVG (_8b5w852Yis [404:20]–[405:20]; [406:00]).
- "8 PM for gold has become like 9:30 for NQ": the "volatility injection" of the new Asia session (_8b5w852Yis [403:40]–[404:00]).
- The model "does not care" about obvious liquidity such as equal highs. It only cares where the open manipulates (_8b5w852Yis [403:40]).
- Target about 2R. "Pair it with bubbles": take profit near strong opposing selling pressure. Stop with "a little room" beyond the inversion (_8b5w852Yis [407:20]–[408:20]).
- Nuance: below VWAP he would expect downside continuation, "but the model prints, I'm going to take the trade". He takes it off if it stalls (_8b5w852Yis [408:40]).
- Silver is the correlated pair. If silver is "melting", he is wary of a gold long even when the model prints (_8b5w852Yis [406:40]).
- Scope and results he claims: "it has to be gold", silver "sometimes"; backtested at the NY open it is weaker; "in over 14 gold sessions, this has only lost twice"; one trade within the first 20 minutes; four days green (Jan) and an 11-day green streak on a dedicated gold account (Feb) (_8b5w852Yis [408:00]–[408:20]; [412:20]–[412:40]; sl4Phv8Gadc [167:20]–[167:40]).
- Gold/silver scalp: when gold sweeps a 15-minute low but silver (the stronger asset) is only at the midpoint of its prior 15-minute candle, he scalps gold back into the range (sl4Phv8Gadc [169:00]–[169:20]).

## 3. Stops, targets, management

- Base hits over home runs. He says Kane's $2M run "was base hits" (Pcv1ZQg7LyM [164:00]). His own trade: 70 points short and "no need to risk it... if this thing runs another 100 points, I don't have FOMO anymore" (_8b5w852Yis [391:40]; [394:40]).
- During the 100K/100-day run, "I never once took full TP". He exits "10 points before, 15 points before... can I always get 60% of that target? Almost always" (cZPjYzTO0gw [599:20]–[600:00]).
