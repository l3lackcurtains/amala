## 2. Setup criteria & entry triggers

**The two (or three) models:**
- Asked how many setups he has: "two or three" (LXINkzt6kIc [05:20]).
- **Model 1, look-below/above-and-fail (LBF/LAF) at the value edge (balance):** "If we're in a balance, I'm looking at areas where we break under the value area low/high, developing value area low/high, reclaim to go long/short of that value area, take you to the other side of balance." (ta0J2nAv2oA [164:20])
- **Model 2, breakout from value and retest of the LVN (trend):** "I look for breakouts from value and a retest of the low volume nodes. Retest back into the low volume nodes." (LXINkzt6kIc [46:40]) "If we've broken out of balance, we create low volume pockets… look for a[n] entry around there." (ta0J2nAv2oA [164:20])
- He calls the retest model "part of my model… It's a retest model. You have little volume pockets… you see some absorption… you have trapped buying, all that positive delta, back down." (5C8Wx_oqfHA [131:00]–[131:20])
- **Exhaustion play** (a third type seen on stream): after a 146-point leg down with heavy selling, he goes long on signs of exhaustion. Short-covering is buy-side fuel. "Exhaustion plays I'm usually looking for 25–30 points… and a quick move." (5C8Wx_oqfHA [111:20]–[112:00], [161:40])
- The "third model" question got a joke answer ("It's ICT, bro… Busted"), so there is no real third model in this batch (LXINkzt6kIc [54:20]).

**How LAF/LBF is defined (his explanation):**
- Price pushes outside value or above an LVN and fails. "In one candle [it] just wiped all that value out with momentum… holding that resistance, the low volume area." That is "look above and fail", and it "usually means I'll look for a move to the other side of value" (5C8Wx_oqfHA [140:20]–[142:40]).
- He uses the developing value as the reference. "This is your developing value, the pink line… Do we look below? Are we going to reclaim?… We did trap some selling here. Lot of negative delta down here. This is your edge of your volume profile value area." (ta0J2nAv2oA [100:00]–[100:40])
- Confirmation he wants for a real look-below: "a lot of sellers getting trapped. A steep… strong move down and then a reclaim". A shallow look below doesn't qualify (Lsa6uOrfAnQ [102:40]–[103:00]).
- Example: "Sweep up. Look above, failed. Rejection. Added the first position early. Once it rejected, I got in." (5C8Wx_oqfHA [28:40])
- He sometimes builds the position early ("in case it doesn't come up and fall back below… I want to have some exposure"), then adds on the rejection (5C8Wx_oqfHA [20:20]–[20:40], [28:40]).

**What makes a level a point of interest (POI):**
- "Look at where your weak pocket is. Look at where the big trades are. Look at where your delta cluster is built… This is a point of interest now cuz we're seeing transactions coming in there, pauses, pushes." (ta0J2nAv2oA [34:40]–[35:20])
- "My strategy is essentially… using order flow levels or levels confirmed by order flow and volume profile to confirm support and resistance for entry." (LXINkzt6kIc [48:20])
- Levels emerge during the day from flow. "We found this level in the morning based on the flow that was coming in and we're seeing activity there… Same level, same pivot." (5C8Wx_oqfHA [128:00])
- "Is it random that this is just an area that I marked?… You have big trades there. Delta building out there… That's what forms the big areas or the level of interest." (5C8Wx_oqfHA [133:40]–[134:00])
- A level that held through tons of big orders and then a breakout retest is confirmed support. Once broken and failed from below, it can become resistance (LXINkzt6kIc [48:40]–[49:00]).
- The 30-second ORB is a pivot, not a stand-alone trigger: "I use it as a pivot. It comes back and see if there's confluence there, if there's big trades happening there." (Lsa6uOrfAnQ [35:20])
- Previous day low as a level: at PDL he wants a sweep below, big trades coming in, and selling trapped below, then a reclaim (ta0J2nAv2oA [106:40]–[107:00]).
- Overnight high and low act as sweep levels. "I want to see if you can sweep this overnight high… see if it comes below this 940 area once you sweep." (Lsa6uOrfAnQ [32:40])
- He watches relative equal lows inside value for reaction (HyU62IlYkY8 [40:00]).

**Delta: trapped traders vs absorption:**
- "It's important to me once it comes into a point of interest… I'm watching for two things: trapped buying or selling, or absorption." (LXINkzt6kIc [61:00]–[61:20])
- **Trapped selling:** "You see a lot of negative delta coming in. You have a strong move down and the next 30 seconds, 40 seconds, all that negative delta… we sweep up. Buyers take control. We get above that negative delta and holding above." Trapped buying is the mirror image (LXINkzt6kIc [61:20]–[61:40]).
- **Absorption:** "A lot of positive delta coming in, but price is just not going up. It's just stalling out. There's like 200, 300 positive delta built up… a bar with a positive 3, 400 delta, but price is just not going up… there's a passive seller there absorbing all those buy orders." (LXINkzt6kIc [62:00]–[62:40])
- Delta threshold: "It's not over 500. That's what I consider a lot." (HyU62IlYkY8 [91:00])
- Delta clusters cited as significant on stream: -1,000 at a tick for the whole session (HyU62IlYkY8 [37:00]); -1,700 at a level (ta0J2nAv2oA [110:00]); +1,268 / +1,200 trapped buys at an LVN (5C8Wx_oqfHA [114:40], [124:00]); +400 at 599 (ta0J2nAv2oA [115:00]); +354 at one tick (Lsa6uOrfAnQ [65:20]); "negative thousand" stalling (ta0J2nAv2oA [94:40]).
- Delta is not proof of buyer or seller. "Does that necessarily mean it's a buyer? No… We can use the market context, how it's acting around those levels… accelerate through… impulse legs." (ta0J2nAv2oA [96:20]–[97:00])
- Delta can come without big trades: "maybe cuz they're selling, loading in small increments instead of huge increments." (HyU62IlYkY8 [42:00])
- Delta at a level "doesn't magically disappear". The chart delta profile resets every 30 minutes, so after a reset you check whether new delta is printing at the same area (HyU62IlYkY8 [64:20]–[65:00]).
- Reading a reloading seller: "I'm not interested in longs… until we can really get over 152. There's a seller sitting here. Reloading at 52s. That's how I'm interpreting it." (HyU62IlYkY8 [40:20]–[40:40])

**How LVNs form and what he wants at them:**
