## 2. Setup criteria & entry triggers

### Volume profile / value framework
- Balanced market = zig-zag, no follow-through, profile looks like a bell curve; this is when you use volume profile and wait for the edges (CsfJpNSL6Kk [16:00]–[16:20]).
- Value area = where 70% of the volume traded; buyers and sellers agree on price inside (CsfJpNSL6Kk [19:20], [22:40]–[23:00], [28:00]).
- Middle of value = chop, "no trade"; best trades happen at the edges where both sides fight it out (CsfJpNSL6Kk [28:00]–[29:00]).
- Conflicting structure (lower highs + higher lows) = compression; that is when volume profile becomes the deciding tool (CsfJpNSL6Kk [15:00]–[15:20], [16:20]).
- Short at a value edge: price breaks above VAH and comes back underneath; wait for a lower high to form to join the sellers (CsfJpNSL6Kk [18:40]–[19:00]).
- Aggressive variant: short right as price slams back inside value without waiting for a retest = a "momentum trade" (CsfJpNSL6Kk [25:20]–[26:20]).
- Trapped-trader mechanic: trapped longs stopping out add selling pressure and fuel the move the other way (CsfJpNSL6Kk [25:40]–[26:00]).
- Look above and fail: value formed inside, buyers attempt a breakout higher, price gets back underneath and holds = trapped buying; target the POC / other side of the range (lAqRPLhLAwM [26:00]–[26:20], [29:00]).
- Failed-breakout re-entry: after a breakdown, a strong breakout back inside the week's value with strong volume; enter on the retest, stop just underneath (outside value in case it was a false breakout) (lAqRPLhLAwM [18:40]–[19:00]).
- Retest of a broken support after news: watch whether buyers defend and the selling below becomes trapped; risk 3–4 points to make ~20 (ES) (lAqRPLhLAwM [21:40]–[22:20]).
- LBF example at a level cluster: developing VAL + yesterday's open + yesterday's POC + yesterday's VAL stacked; look under and quickly reclaim the developing VAL = long "scout" in a rangebound day (Q84I7xDYsNs [36:40]–[37:20]).
