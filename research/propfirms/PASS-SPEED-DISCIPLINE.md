  variant 3 days + 40%, $3K. Payout request resets MLL to $0 (rebuild ~$3K before next withdrawable).

- Apex: 1–2 day pass possible (promo); $3K target/$2K EOD-trailing 50K; funded: min 5 qualifying days,
  $250 EOD per-account min profit, $500 min request, balance floor ≈$52,600, 100% first $25K then 90%,

  [mixed Company/Anecdotal].
- Tradeify: Growth 1-day pass (no eval consistency), Select 3 days/40%; first payout ≥5 profitable days

## 4. Compounding math (own arithmetic, labeled calculated)
$50K/$3K target/$2K trailing floor, $200–600/day: 15–25 sessions at $200/day (net), 6–8 at $600; 3×−$600
or 5×−$400 ends the eval. Band: weeks-to-months per attempt; ×2–3 attempts; + stage gates → first payout
6–12 weeks (skilled) / 4–12 months (typical); Reddit medians 3–9 months to first payout, 1–3 yr to
consistency.

## 5. Discipline evidence
### Academic
- Barber & Odean, JF 2000 (66,465 households 1991–96) [Academic]: most-active quintile 11.4%/yr vs
  market 17.9%; trading lost to same households' own held stocks. >6,000 citations.
- Chagué, De-Losso, Giovannetti (Brazil CVM, 19,646 mini-index day traders 2013–15) [Academic]: 80% quit
  within 2 years; of survivors past 300 days, 97% lost money net of fees; ~0.4% earned > ~$54/day net;
  top earner avg $310/day w/ $2,560 SD; NO evidence of learning-by-doing.
- Barber/Lee/Liu/Odean RFS 2010 (1,383 Taiwanese day traders): 1,365 lost; ~1% earned a risk-adjusted
  living; persistence ≠ skill.
- Disposition effect: Shefrin & Statman 1985; Odean 1998: winners realized ~2.5× more often than losers;
  kept losers outperform sold winners → maps to trailing-floor violations.
- Villahermosa, "Prop-Firm Challenges: A Barrier Model" SSRN Sep 2026 (1,713 NQ sessions, 1-min) +
  Trading Wyckoff writeup [Working paper + author's live ledger]:
  skillless geometry rate L/(T+L) ≈ 44.4% → 40.4% with forced flatten → 34.7% with trailing DD ("winning
  does not buy room — it lifts the floor"); 40% funded-consistency rule cuts the funnel more than the
  eval (removing 2-day pass: 35%→21%); every withdrawal thins the cushion until the frozen floor kills
  the account — "most accounts die payout after payout"; reinvesting payouts vs withdrawing: 25.5% vs
  ~12% runs dead in 3 years (5-account sim); working capital $1,500–3,000 minimizes ruin; every instant-
  funding tier studied loses money on average. Author ledger: 29 challenges → 12 funded → 5 ever paid
  (+$1,771 net) ≈ matches 12% model.
### Prop-specific
- ~71% of first-phase failures attributed to daily-loss-limit breaches (HOC-Trade, 500K+ analyzed)
  [Vendor claim, denominator unpublished — flag].
- Topstep Responsible Trading rule text: max position, letting losers run, no stops, "full port, trade on
  tilt, FOMO, or revenge", disrespecting the daily limit; DLL now = lockout not failure (discipline
  device); journal by rule-followed.
- FPFX: killers are 2nd/3rd trades taken specifically to recover the first, sized larger than plan.
- Revenge quotes [Anecdotal]: "can't tell you how many accounts I've lost to revenge trading"; payout
  delay → "tilt traded and blew the account" (r/Daytrading Apex thread).
- Payout-day psychology: house-money sizing after payouts; fear-of-denial hoarding leaving profits
  exposed [ThePropFirmGuide Independent-with-affiliate-bias].
### Tools (recurring)
Personal stop below firm DLL (−$400 vs −$1,000/−$2,000) + platform lockout; size from worst realistic
streak; trade-count caps; rule-tracking sheets w/ self-penalties; 15-min step-away; rest days; journal
emotions 2–3 wks to find the trigger state; firm-native enforcement (resets limited 2/day Topstep).

## 6. Patience quantified
- r/Forex 10,000-sim FTMO Monte Carlo (code shared) [A but reproducible]: same edge (50% WR, 1.5R/1R,
  5/day) — 93% pass at 0.5% risk vs 9% at 2.0%.
- LuxAlgo Topstep sim [Vendor tool]: 38.1% (4/day @1%) → 71.0% (@0.75%) → 95.6% (3/day @0.5%). Ordering
  is the finding.
- Zero-edge coin flip (AlgoLift): $100/day → 14% pass, 189 median days; $300 → 27%, 49; $800 → 10 days,
  ruin explodes. "Trading small costs time, not much else — there is no clock; the monthly fee punishes it."
- Villahermosa: "you do not pass by having a great day, you pass by having two identical ones."
- Mark Douglas (via Synapse summary) [self-help]: <10% consistent winners; 40–50% boom-bust; size so 20
  losses survivable. FTMO blog winner example [Vendor]: +20.8%/month on low WR, ~$1,800 avg win vs ~$520
  avg loss.
- GAP: no peer-reviewed study of A+/session-filter effects on prop rule sets. Label sim numbers
  "simulation-illustrated."

## 7. Contradictions
1. 16.8% vs 51.8% vs "3.5% paid": denominators — both true; read the denominator.
2. FPFX 7% ever-paid vs Topstep 33.3%-of-funded-paid vs Goat 3.7% vs PipFarm "1 in 4": different
   populations (buyers vs funded) — label, don't blend.
3. Speedrun culture (r/TopStepX "pass in 2 days, resets cheap") vs every sim/academic finding
   (aggressive sizing = 83% killer); same forum's veterans took 18+ months.
4. Apex "we pay millions" ledger vs 2026 payout-purgatory denials: pays many-small reliably, large
   contestably.
5. Instant-funding marketing vs barrier-model finding that instant tiers lose money on average [contested].
6. Third-party "industry averages" are models trained on the same few disclosures — circular; label as
   estimates.
7. Tradecovex "TopstepX pass rates 86% higher" — platform claim, no methodology; do NOT publish as fact.
