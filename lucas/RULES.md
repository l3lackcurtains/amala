## 1. Entry Rules

1. **Wait for the candle close.** "Do not jump the gun. Wait for the actual candle close." (being-right-doesnt-make-you-money) — entering a bear flag early cost traders a 35–40 point wick against them before the move went their way.
2. **Strict checklist — no entry until every box is checked.** "You must have a strict checklist. It doesn't matter if your checklist is different than mine; what matters is that you refuse to enter a trade until *every single box* is checked." (being-right-doesnt-make-you-money)
3. **His confluence checklist (V-shape/flag entries):** (are-you-skipping-this-step-my-nq-trading-checklist)
   - Sweep of a prominent low/high (liquidity taken out),
   - Stagnant/exhausted price action after the sweep,
   - V-shape recovery (or rollover) + clear bull/bear flag,
   - Flag candle closes above (or below) the EMAs,
   - Only then: entry.
4. **A+ bear-flag checklist** (my-a-bear-flag-checklist-for-140-point-nq-setups): bias from failed retest of internal high → price staying below VWAP and no 5-min closes above EMAs → targets mapped (internal wick lows + session lows) → clear flag → entry on flag break to a new low → stop at the flag's top wick.
5. **No blind buying:** "If the market spiked up, I'd just blindly hit the 'buy' button out of FOMO. That isn't a setup; that is just gambling." (what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)
6. **9:25 AM NY-open decision rule:** hold above VWAP at the bell → target London Highs; reject VWAP → short the deep pre-market lows. (my-pre-market-routine-for-the-new-york-open)
7. **No trading in "No Man's Land"** — the ~50 minutes between the Globex open (6 PM EST) and the true Asia open. Liquidity is too thin, stops get blown through. (what-i-look-for-in-the-asia-session)

## 2. Sizing Rules

1. **Size so a normal wick can't kill you:** "If your size is so heavy that a standard 20-point wick blows your account, you are trading too big." (being-right-doesnt-make-you-money)
2. **Size down after losses:** "When you take a massive loss, you have zero conviction left in yourself. The only way to rebuild that confidence is by stacking up micro-wins." — he sized "all the way down" to rebuild. (trading-full-time-heres-why-you-need-a-second-income)
3. **Recalibrate size after every payout:** "When your drawdown buffer shrinks after a withdrawal, you *have* to recalibrate your contract sizing and risk parameters." After his $25K payout he tried to full-port for $7–8K/day — "Spoiler alert: it did not work out for me." (why-you-forget-how-to-trade-after-a-payout)
4. **Scale laterally, not vertically:** keep 1 contract per account, add more funded accounts on a copy trader instead of bigger size. "Do not randomly start trading 10 contracts." (what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)
5. **Beginner sizing:** "your position sizing should be much smaller than you think it should be" while testing a strategy on an eval. (if-i-had-to-start-trading-over)

## 3. Adding / Averaging Rules (DCA)

From adding-to-a-trade-when-your-in-drawdown-my-exact-strategy:

1. **Only average into a trend, never against it** — clear prior trend (bias), an obvious target beyond, and a hard stop-loss are prerequisites. No blind dip-buying.
2. **Add when price makes a new lower low (fake-out) but the macro trend and hard stop are intact** — add at least the original contract count (2 → +2) to pull the average 10+ points.
3. **Add again on retest ONLY if the candle does not close below the previous established low.** "As long as that floor holds, you are safe to hold for your original target."
4. **Bottom wicks in a row at support = bullish confluence** that justifies holding the averaged position.
5. The hard stop is sacred — if it runs, the DCA thesis is dead.

## 4. Drawdown / Stop Rules

1. **Hard stops at structure:** below the flag wick low (long) / top wick of flag (short) / nearest wick low or candle body (scalps). Never arbitrary.
2. **Give the market room:** "You have to give the market enough space to fluctuate between drawdown and profit." (being-right-doesnt-make-you-money)
3. **Stop to break-even after the first target:** "immediately move your stop loss to break-even (or slightly into profit)." (my-a-bear-flag-checklist)
4. **Cutting stops early is a symptom of over-leverage need:** "If you are cutting your stop early, that money means more to you than it should." (the-brutal-reality-of-trading-when-youre-broke)
5. **Moving your stop out of fear "works" sometimes — that's the trap:** it's how "your P&L reinforces terrible habits and lies to you." (why-your-pl-is-lying-to-you)

## 5. Take-Profit / Payout Rules & Habits

1. **Targets are pre-marked wick lows/highs and session levels** — never guessed. In open air, target the nearest major psychological level (e.g., 30,500).
2. **Scale out in tranches:** contract 1 at the first internal wick level, contract 2 at the next major level, contract 3 held as a runner for the deepest level.
3. **Take profit slightly BEFORE pivot targets** to guarantee fills: "Do not be greedy! What's a few missed points if you secure a massive win?" (what-i-look-for-in-the-asia-session)
4. **Lock in early on extended moves:** "we locked in our profits early — better safe than sorry." (the-5-trades)
5. **Trust the final target over quick profit:** even flashing +$1,300 doesn't override the mapped target if TA points lower. (the-setup-i-use-for-every-nq-breakout)
6. **Withdraw consistently and small:** his $24,000 week was built from many small payouts — Tradeify: $1,400 + $1,200 + $2,500 + $700 + $700 + $700 + $691 (~$8,000); Lucid: $8,000 + four ~$2,400 payout requests (~$9,600). He runs payouts across many accounts rather than letting balances pile up.
7. **After any payout: recalibrate size down, drop to A+ setups only, rebuild rhythm with small wins.** (why-you-forget-how-to-trade-after-a-payout)

## 6. Eval / Account Rules

1. **Set a strict monthly budget for eval purchases:** "Set a strict monthly budget for how many evaluation accounts you are allowed to buy. Keep everything in moderation and track what you are spending." Only scale up after definitively proving the process repeats. (if-i-had-to-start-trading-over)
2. **Never brute-force accounts:** "When I was blowing accounts, I didn't set a limit. I just bought as many as possible and tried to brute-force my way into profitability. Do not do this!" (same article)
3. **Never fund evals with debt:** "I opened credit cards and maxed them out buying trading accounts with no job and no way to pay the money back. *(Do not do this. It is the dumbest thing you can possibly do.)*" (how-i-became-a-full-time-trader)
4. **Skin in the game matters:** move from sim to funded/micros once the strategy proves out — "$160 buying an evaluation account, that is 8 hours of your real life you just spent. You will trade that account much more cautiously."
5. **Use real risk to pass evals the same way you trade funded:** his Vest 10K eval was passed in 9 minutes by trading his normal short setup on a trend day (target $1,000), not by gambling.
6. **Prefer firms/rules that don't constrain his style:** he highlights WarBux's no payout caps, no news restrictions, and no forced move to live — freedom rules matter to his multi-account approach. (this-crypto-prop-firm-that-lets-you-keep-100)

## 7. "When To Stop" Rules

1. **Don't trade the chop days at all:** big overnight move + indecisive open candle + barcoding on EMAs + wicks everywhere + no clear targets = no trading that day ("hide the power cable"). (the-days-you-shouldnt-trade-nq-at-all)
2. **Some funded-account days get zero trades:** "Some days I won't trade them at all. I wait for my specific setup." (what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)
3. **After 2–3 fake-outs in a range, stop taking the 4th:** "Stop trying to force a setup in a dead market!" (the-days-you-shouldnt-trade-nq-at-all)
4. **Don't force trades to buy things:** the $18K loss happened because he forced trades "to squeeze just a little bit more out of the market to pay for" a purchase he wanted. (i-lost-18000-in-a-single-day)

## 8. Daily Routine (framed as rules)

1. **Same pre-market ritual every day:** indicators on (9 EMA / 21 EMA / VWAP / Kill Zones & Pivots), mark obvious wick highs and lows on BOTH sides of price ~9:25 AM, define the VWAP-based if/then plan before 9:30. "This is the exact routine I do every single day before the New York open and before hopping on stream for the Asia session." (my-pre-market-routine-for-the-new-york-open)
2. **Asia routine:** at 6 PM Globex open, mark the initial prominent highs/lows immediately; wait out No Man's Land; trade only flags with VWAP + EMA confluence after the Asia open.
3. **Discipline routine that fixed his consistency:** "I started waking up at 4:00 AM, doing push-ups on my balcony, tracking all my calories, going to the gym twice a day, and strictly limiting myself to just **one or two trades a day**." (how-i-became-a-full-time-trader)
4. **One setup, repeated:** after a payout or a win, "reverse engineer your success" — write down the exact setups, trade count, and mindset, "and do nothing but repeat it." (what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)

## 9. Money / Lifestyle Rules

1. **Always keep a second income:** "You should always have a separate, guaranteed income outside of trading." His benchmark: a valet job at $25/hr → "$150 guaranteed per six-hour shift... $700–$800 a week" covers living costs so trading is pressure-free. (the-brutal-reality-of-trading-when-youre-broke; trading-full-time-heres-why-you-need-a-second-income)
2. **Budget lifestyle off your BASELINE month, not your best month:** he "started basing my entire lifestyle and spending habits off my absolute *best* trading months, instead of my baseline, consistent monthly average" — and calls it a core mistake. (i-lost-18000-in-a-single-day)
3. **Don't convert P&L into price tags:** stop mentally pricing purchases in trading "points."
4. **Start on ES, then NQ; one ticker only:** "Pick one ticker and master how it moves." (if-i-had-to-start-trading-over)

## The 10 Rules In Short (his operating system)

1. Mark obvious wick highs/lows + session levels before every session; both sides of price.
2. Trade only bull/bear flags at 9 EMA / 21 EMA / VWAP confluence — after the candle close.
3. Never trade No Man's Land, chop days, or after a big overnight trend.
4. Hard stops at flag structure; risk sized so a 20-point wick can't matter.
5. Scale out at pre-marked levels; BE stop after target 1; TP just before pivots.
6. DCA only with trend + target + hard stop; add on new lows, never if a candle closes below the prior low.
7. 1 contract per account; scale by adding accounts, not size.
8. Monthly eval budget; never debt; recalibrate size after every payout.
9. One or two trades a day; reverse-engineer winning days and repeat them exactly.
10. Keep a second income; live off your baseline month, never your best month.
