## The Golden Rule: One Trade Per Account Per Day

1. **One trade per account, per day.** His self-described "ultimate rule," reinstated after blowing two Blue Sky accounts by chasing what everyone else at the desk was doing (one-trade-per-account-per-day-why-i-walk-away). With 20+ funded accounts that means maybe five total trades a day: focus on whichever accounts are closest to hitting a payout or passing an evaluation, take the one trade, and then physically walk away from the screens. *"If you are up for the day, take the money and walk away. If you took a loss, accept it and walk away."*
   > "**One trade per account, per day.** If the trade is a win? I am done with that account for the day. If the trade is a loss? I am done with that account for the day. No revenge trading. No looking for 'just one more setup' to make the money back. Once an account has taken a trade, it is locked for the next 24 hours."

## Risk Management

2. **Always use a stop loss.** The one time he skipped it cost him a live account plus $3,000 of his own money: *"I broke the absolute number one rule of trading: I did not put a freaking stop loss"* (i-lost-my-live-account-and-my-own-3000).
3. **Define risk before entry.** Stops go at structural points — one tick beyond the trigger-candle wick (Two in the Chamber), the other side of the Globex first candle, below the round-number level, or just below a previously tagged session level. He uses TradingView's Long/Short Position tool to map SL/TP before entry (this-is-exactly-how-i-set-up-my-tradingview-chart).
4. **Keep risk-to-reward disciplined.** "Build a risk-to-reward profile that actually works for your personality and account size, and respect the stops" (why-most-traders-fail). Standard profiles: strict 1:1 on reversals, 2:1 on the Globex setup (risk ~$350 → target $700).
5. **Cut losses early.** He manually cut a 20-point stop to save capital (lost-900); *"held onto losing trades instead of cutting them early"* is listed as a rule break (i-broke-every-rule-i-have-today).
6. **Never add to losers.** Adding to a losing news trade pushed him into the $1,000 daily loss limit (i-tried-to-trade-the-news).
7. **Never size up out of emotion.** After a -$200 loss he loaded 5 contracts on a 300K Apex account for a revenge win — *"Real talk: I am apologizing in advance for that. You should not trade like that… you should always stick to your sizing rules!"* (i-broke-my-own-rules-and-it-worked).
8. **Scale down on small live accounts.** With a $2,000-drawdown live account or a personal account under $10,000, "you have to scale way down. **Stick to 5 micros maximum. Do not load up the minis.**" (i-lost-my-live-account-and-my-own-3000).

## Walking Away

9. **Know exactly when to walk away from the charts.** This is his core concept: overtrading → blowing accounts → tilt → destroyed mental state (one-trade-per-account-per-day).
10. **Close the laptop when the market chops.** *"If the market is chopping around and you don't feel like staring at the screen anymore, close the laptop and walk away. The market will always be there tomorrow"* (why-most-traders-fail).
11. **Stop trading after securing the day.** He took the $2,000 comeback trade and "That was my cue to stop trading and lock in the profits for the night" (every-trade-i-took-on-a-bearish-nq-night).
12. **Cap screen time at one to two hours.** His pre-BDH habit he is returning to: *"I traded maybe one to two hours a day, max, and then I was completely done"* (one-trade-per-account-per-day).

## When NOT to Trade (His Explicit No-Trade Conditions)

13. **No news trades.** "Normally, I don't trade the news whatsoever. Waiting for the news release to settle is always the smarter move." A news-candle gamble hit his $1,000 daily loss limit, and a news-candle long cost -$4,000 and blew two Apex accounts: "it's a textbook example of why you don't play around with news candles. You will lose your money fast" (i-tried-to-trade-the-news; i-broke-every-rule-i-have-today).
14. **No election or major-event nights.** "I shouldn't have been trading on election night, plain and simple" — the open whip-sawed his long out for an instant -$700 (why-i-shouldnt-have-traded-election-night).
15. **Don't trade chop you can't play.** "If you don't know how to play the range, don't force it. Just don't trade it at all" (how-i-trade-chop); he sat out 6:40–7:45 AM in a messy range rather than force trades (i-tried-to-trade-the-news).
16. **Skip Globex 6–8 PM for the reversal setup.** It is "notoriously choppy… I avoid it entirely" (2-in-the-chamber).
17. **Only click when an A+ setup exists.** "Where I excel is refusing to click buttons when my setup isn't there… Forcing a trade just for action is piss-poor management and piss-poor discipline" (why-most-traders-fail).
18. **Respect the daily-loss-limit freeze.** After breaking the daily loss limit, soft-breach accounts freeze for the day — he respects the freeze ("paused until tomorrow").

## Prop Firm Drawdown Management (His Educational Framework)

19. **Treat the drawdown as your real account size.** "The 'Account Size' is basically just a marketing label. **The drawdown is your real account size.**" Real loss budgets: 25K = $1,000; 50K = $2,000; 100K = $3,000; 150K = $4,500 (dont-buy-a-prop-account-until-you-watch-this).
20. **Know your drawdown type.** **Static:** the floor never moves up — profits increase real room. **EOD trailing:** the floor recalculates at end of day — you never gain extra room. **Trailing on unrealized gains** is the danger case.
21. **Take profits when trading a trailing drawdown.** *"Take your profits when trading a trailing drawdown!"* — an open +$500 peak that closes at break-even still consumes $500 of drawdown (dont-buy-a-prop-account-until-you-watch-this).
22. **Choose intraday vs EOD trail deliberately.** EOD is "much safer" but costs double (apex-changed-every-rule).
23. **Always build a buffer, even when the firm doesn't require one.** "If you take out all your profit and your drawdown trailing floor has moved up, you could instantly blow your account… Always build a buffer first to protect your account, even if the firm doesn't strictly require it!" (iq-capital-review).
24. **Respect consistency rules.** He stopped trading an IQ Capital account at +$988 (target was passable) "to respect their consistency rule" (one-long-turned-my-worst-morning-green). He avoids plans with brutal consistency — Tradeify Lightning's 20%: "I absolutely hate that rule, which is why I don't personally trade this account."
25. **Know soft vs hard breaches.** A soft daily loss limit freezes the account for the day; a hard breach terminates it (vest-markets, ftmo-futures-review). This determines which firms he'll trade news-adjacent days on.

## Eval & Payout Strategy

26. **Never hedge on Tradeify.** "If you hedge, your account is banned and it's game over." His easiest-eval ranking (the-easiest-prop-firm-evals-to-pass): Tradeify 50K Growth (no consistency rule, soft $1,250 DLL, news allowed), MFFU Builder (choose $1.5K/$2K DD, soft $1,000 DLL), Lucid Flex 50K (50% consistency → minimum 2 days), Take Profit Trader 50K (no DLL — "If you don't have the discipline to take a loss and walk away, having no daily loss limit means you will blow the entire account in one day").
27. **Run a payout-first mentality.** He took $1,000 off a potential $13,000 runner to lock an IQ Capital payout (i-lost-accounts) and requests payouts immediately after winning sessions ($700 + $700 + $1,000 Tradeify in one night).
28. **Request payouts before you blow.** On Vest he took 4 payouts in one day, lost one account afterward, and the already-requested payouts still cleared — "Because I requested the payouts before blowing the account, the payouts are still going to clear" (i-took-4-payouts-in-one-day).
29. **Cap eval spending at $1,000 a month.** *"I usually try to keep a strict rule of only spending $1,000 max on evaluations per month"* (what-i-spent-on-prop-firms-vs-what-i-made) — a rule he broke in August and acknowledged.
30. **Follow the scaling plan.** Pass evals (1-day where rules allow), 3 profitable days for payout eligibility, stack buffers, request payouts; goal to hold up to 20 funded Apex accounts (how-i-caught-these-3-futures-setups).

## Psychology-Adjacent Rules (Operative in His Workflow)

31. **Mentally accept losing money before every session.** Be able to "sit in your chair for hours doing absolutely nothing while waiting for an A+ setup" (why-most-traders-fail).
32. **Master ONE strategy.** "Stop strategy-hopping… only execute when your exact criteria show up."
33. **Take the money when momentum stalls.** When in a winner and momentum stalls: "take the money and walk away… Never let a solid green trade turn red out of greed — another setup will always come around the corner."
34. **Isolate high-risk experiments.** News gambling goes on a sacrificial single account, never the copied portfolio (i-tried-to-trade-the-news).
35. **Know each firm's specific restrictions.** No hedging (Tradeify), no trading bots (Take Profit Trader), position-size caps (e.g., Tradeify 50K Growth: 4 minis / 40 micros), daily minimum profit days for payout eligibility (Apex: $200/day intraday, $250/day EOD).
