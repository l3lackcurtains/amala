## Entry Rules

1. **Wait for the candle close.** Entering a bear flag early cost traders a 35–40 point wick against them before the move even went their way. *(being-right-doesnt-make-you-money)*
   > "Do not jump the gun. Wait for the actual candle close."
2. **Strict checklist — no entry until every box is checked.** *(being-right-doesnt-make-you-money)*
   > "You must have a strict checklist. It doesn't matter if your checklist is different than mine; what matters is that you refuse to enter a trade until *every single box* is checked."
3. **His confluence checklist (V-shape/flag entries).** The full sequence he runs before any entry: *(are-you-skipping-this-step-my-nq-trading-checklist)*
   - Sweep of a prominent low/high (liquidity taken out),
   - Stagnant/exhausted price action after the sweep,
   - V-shape recovery (or rollover) + clear bull/bear flag,
   - Flag candle closes above (or below) the EMAs,
   - Only then: entry.
4. **A+ bear-flag checklist.** Bias from failed retest of internal high → price staying below VWAP and no 5-min closes above EMAs → targets mapped (internal wick lows + session lows) → clear flag → entry on flag break to a new low → stop at the flag's top wick. *(my-a-bear-flag-checklist-for-140-point-nq-setups)*
5. **No blind buying.** FOMO spikes are not setups. *(what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)*
   > "If the market spiked up, I'd just blindly hit the 'buy' button out of FOMO. That isn't a setup; that is just gambling."
6. **9:25 AM NY-open decision rule.** Hold above VWAP at the bell → target the London Highs; reject VWAP → short the deep pre-market lows. *(my-pre-market-routine-for-the-new-york-open)*
7. **No trading in "No Man's Land"** — the ~50 minutes between the Globex open (6 PM EST) and the true Asia open. Liquidity is too thin, stops get blown through. *(what-i-look-for-in-the-asia-session)*

## Sizing Rules

1. **Size so a normal wick can't kill you.** *(being-right-doesnt-make-you-money)*
   > "If your size is so heavy that a standard 20-point wick blows your account, you are trading too big."
2. **Size down after losses.** He sized "all the way down" to rebuild. *(trading-full-time-heres-why-you-need-a-second-income)*
   > "When you take a massive loss, you have zero conviction left in yourself. The only way to rebuild that confidence is by stacking up micro-wins."
3. **Recalibrate size after every payout.** After his $25K payout he tried to full-port for $7–8K/day — "Spoiler alert: it did not work out for me." *(why-you-forget-how-to-trade-after-a-payout)*
   > "When your drawdown buffer shrinks after a withdrawal, you *have* to recalibrate your contract sizing and risk parameters."
4. **Scale laterally, not vertically.** Keep 1 contract per account, add more funded accounts on a copy trader instead of bigger size. "Do not randomly start trading 10 contracts." *(what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)*
5. **Beginner sizing.** While testing a strategy on an eval: *(if-i-had-to-start-trading-over)*
   > "Your position sizing should be much smaller than you think it should be."

## Adding / Averaging Rules (DCA)

From *adding-to-a-trade-when-your-in-drawdown-my-exact-strategy*:

1. **Only average into a trend, never against it.** A clear prior trend (bias), an obvious target beyond, and a hard stop-loss are prerequisites. No blind dip-buying.
2. **Add when price makes a new lower low (fake-out) but the macro trend and hard stop are intact.** Add at least the original contract count (2 → +2) to pull the average 10+ points.
3. **Add again on retest ONLY if the candle does not close below the previous established low.**
   > "As long as that floor holds, you are safe to hold for your original target."
4. **Bottom wicks in a row at support = bullish confluence** that justifies holding the averaged position.
5. **The hard stop is sacred.** If it runs, the DCA thesis is dead.

## Drawdown / Stop Rules

1. **Hard stops at structure.** Below the flag wick low (long) / top wick of flag (short) / nearest wick low or candle body (scalps). Never arbitrary.
2. **Give the market room.** *(being-right-doesnt-make-you-money)*
   > "You have to give the market enough space to fluctuate between drawdown and profit."
3. **Stop to break-even after the first target.** *(my-a-bear-flag-checklist)*
   > "Immediately move your stop loss to break-even (or slightly into profit)."
4. **Cutting stops early is a symptom of needing the money.** *(the-brutal-reality-of-trading-when-youre-broke)*
   > "If you are cutting your stop early, that money means more to you than it should."
5. **Moving your stop out of fear "works" sometimes — that's the trap.** It's how "your P&L reinforces terrible habits and lies to you." *(why-your-pl-is-lying-to-you)*

## Take-Profit / Payout Rules & Habits

1. **Targets are pre-marked wick lows/highs and session levels** — never guessed. In open air, target the nearest major psychological level (e.g., 30,500).
2. **Scale out in tranches.** Contract 1 at the first internal wick level, contract 2 at the next major level, contract 3 held as a runner for the deepest level.
3. **Take profit slightly BEFORE pivot targets** to guarantee fills. *(what-i-look-for-in-the-asia-session)*
   > "Do not be greedy! What's a few missed points if you secure a massive win?"
4. **Lock in early on extended moves.** *(the-5-trades)*
   > "We locked in our profits early — better safe than sorry."
5. **Trust the final target over quick profit.** Even flashing +$1,300 doesn't override the mapped target if TA points lower. *(the-setup-i-use-for-every-nq-breakout)*
6. **Withdraw consistently and small.** His $24,000 week was built from many small payouts — Tradeify: $1,400 + $1,200 + $2,500 + $700 + $700 + $700 + $691 (~$8,000); Lucid: $8,000 + four ~$2,400 payout requests (~$9,600). He runs payouts across many accounts rather than letting balances pile up.
7. **After any payout: recalibrate size down, drop to A+ setups only, rebuild rhythm with small wins.** *(why-you-forget-how-to-trade-after-a-payout)*

## Eval / Account Rules

1. **Set a strict monthly budget for eval purchases.** Only scale up after definitively proving the process repeats. *(if-i-had-to-start-trading-over)*
   > "Set a strict monthly budget for how many evaluation accounts you are allowed to buy. Keep everything in moderation and track what you are spending."
2. **Never brute-force accounts.** *(if-i-had-to-start-trading-over)*
   > "When I was blowing accounts, I didn't set a limit. I just bought as many as possible and tried to brute-force my way into profitability. Do not do this!"
3. **Never fund evals with debt.** *(how-i-became-a-full-time-trader)*
   > "I opened credit cards and maxed them out buying trading accounts with no job and no way to pay the money back. *(Do not do this. It is the dumbest thing you can possibly do.)*"
4. **Skin in the game matters.** Move from sim to funded/micros once the strategy proves out — "$160 buying an evaluation account, that is 8 hours of your real life you just spent. You will trade that account much more cautiously."
5. **Use real risk to pass evals the same way you trade funded.** His Vest 10K eval was passed in 9 minutes by trading his normal short setup on a trend day (target $1,000), not by gambling.
6. **Prefer firms/rules that don't constrain his style.** He highlights WarBux's no payout caps, no news restrictions, and no forced move to live — freedom rules matter to his multi-account approach. *(this-crypto-prop-firm-that-lets-you-keep-100)*

## "When To Stop" Rules

1. **Don't trade the chop days at all.** Big overnight move + indecisive open candle + barcoding on EMAs + wicks everywhere + no clear targets = no trading that day ("hide the power cable"). *(the-days-you-shouldnt-trade-nq-at-all)*
2. **Some funded-account days get zero trades.** *(what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)*
   > "Some days I won't trade them at all. I wait for my specific setup."
3. **After 2–3 fake-outs in a range, stop taking the 4th.** *(the-days-you-shouldnt-trade-nq-at-all)*
   > "Stop trying to force a setup in a dead market!"
4. **Don't force trades to buy things.** The $18K loss happened because he forced trades "to squeeze just a little bit more out of the market to pay for" a purchase he wanted. *(i-lost-18000-in-a-single-day)*

## Daily Routine (framed as rules)

1. **Same pre-market ritual every day.** Indicators on (9 EMA / 21 EMA / VWAP / Kill Zones & Pivots), mark obvious wick highs and lows on BOTH sides of price ~9:25 AM, define the VWAP-based if/then plan before 9:30. "This is the exact routine I do every single day before the New York open and before hopping on stream for the Asia session." *(my-pre-market-routine-for-the-new-york-open)*
2. **Asia routine.** At 6 PM Globex open, mark the initial prominent highs/lows immediately; wait out No Man's Land; trade only flags with VWAP + EMA confluence after the Asia open.
3. **Discipline routine that fixed his consistency.** *(how-i-became-a-full-time-trader)*
   > "I started waking up at 4:00 AM, doing push-ups on my balcony, tracking all my calories, going to the gym twice a day, and strictly limiting myself to just **one or two trades a day**."
4. **One setup, repeated.** After a payout or a win, "reverse engineer your success" — write down the exact setups, trade count, and mindset, "and do nothing but repeat it." *(what-getting-consistent-payouts-actually-taught-me)*

## Money / Lifestyle Rules

1. **Always keep a second income.** His benchmark: a valet job at $25/hr → "$150 guaranteed per six-hour shift... $700–$800 a week" covers living costs so trading is pressure-free. *(the-brutal-reality-of-trading-when-youre-broke; trading-full-time-heres-why-you-need-a-second-income)*
   > "You should always have a separate, guaranteed income outside of trading."

3. **Don't convert P&L into price tags.** Stop mentally pricing purchases in trading "points."
4. **Start on ES, then NQ; one ticker only.** "Pick one ticker and master how it moves." *(if-i-had-to-start-trading-over)*

## The 10 Rules In Short (his operating system)

1. Mark obvious wick highs/lows + session levels before every session; both sides of price.
2. Trade only bull/bear flags at 9 EMA / 21 EMA / VWAP confluence — after the candle close.
3. Never trade No Man's Land, chop days, or after a big overnight trend.
4. Hard stops at flag structure; risk sized so a 20-point wick can't matter.
5. Scale out at pre-marked levels; BE stop after target 1; TP just before pivots.
6. DCA only with trend + target + hard stop; add on new lows, never if a candle closes below the prior low.
7. 1 contract per account; scale by adding accounts, not size.
8. Monthly eval budget; never debt; recalibrate size after every payout.
9. One or two trades a day; reverse-engineer winning days and repeat them exactly.
10. Keep a second income; live off your baseline month, never your best month.
