## The core system: 60-minute Initial Balance (her "bread and butter")

Her own words, The Day Trading Show podcast (Oct 24, 2026, chapter "Her Initial Balance Strategy Explained" 07:52):

> "So the initial balance is the first 60 minutes of the session and it can be applied to New York, London, Tokyo, whatever. But I trade specifically in the New York session. So from 930 to 1030, you mark off the high and the low of that first 60 minutes. And that's the range of the session. You're using that to kind of set the tone for the day. Um, so basically it's like a 60 minute orb and there's two ways that I trade it either a rejection of the high or the low, which is the fancy term they call it as a failed auction. Um, or you can take a trend continuation trade."

## Setup 2 — Trend Continuation (retrace to IB mid / VWAP)

Podcast: "So like maybe it's a bullish trend and it breaks the IB high initial balance high comes back down and it retraces to like the midpoint or to VWAP. And then you take a trend continuation trade."

Day-type conditional selection — podcast: "It's been more of a trending market. So I'm taking a lot of retracements to the IB mid and trend continuation trades. If it's more choppy, then I'll take the failed auction. So it depends. That's the cool thing about the strategy is you can adjust to the market conditions."

PREREG page: "Setup 2, trend continuation: the IB trended one way; price returns to the IB mid (anchored 09:30 VWAP nearby is extra confluence), rejects, enter with the trend, target the IB low (high), stop beyond the recent swing high (low) [13:34]–[14:36], [15:08]."
